OWN-WORLD: Ink #1C1B19, gold #E2B04A and warm white #FAFAF7. Atelier's object-led typography and sparse surfaces are adapted to the pinned palette; familiar sans controls and tabular figures carry the working UI.

STORY: Describe the piece, compare actual variants, rotate one, inspect weight and quote sources, then edit or download production files. Failures and assumptions remain visible.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact persistent navigation rail, a large studio canvas beside the active brief and primary create action. Completed orders replace the empty canvas with a real render or GLB and a choice of variants. Phone layout puts the object first and navigation in a disclosure.
